  <p>Al Barari Coral opens bright and immediately inviting: bergamot and orange

  natural rather than candy-like. A hint of ginger runs through the opening —
  just enough to give it edge and stop it reading as a simple fruity scent. The
  heart develops gently; orange blossom brings a delicate floral note without
  sweetness, while red berries and cedar add depth and a subtle woody structure
  that anchors the composition. It's a sophisticated treatment of what could
  easily have become a generic summer fragrance.</p>

Projection is polite and close-range, making it an ideal office or daytime option.</p>
